# Synthetic roll fixture

These prices and contract identifiers are generated, not observed. Fixed, predeclared switches every 65 business-day observations create large artificial roll gaps of both signs. The adjusted series must recover the underlying held-contract increments. This demonstrates plumbing and reconciles P&L; it is not evidence of a real-market improvement in VaR.

Real input requires overlapping dated contract settlements, source/usage rights, and an ex-ante schedule respecting expiry and liquidity. Neither Yahoo front-month proxies nor EIA monthly C1-C4 averages can recover that missing contract identity.
